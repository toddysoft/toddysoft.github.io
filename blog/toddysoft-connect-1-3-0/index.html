ToddySoft Connect 1.3.0 Is Out, and a Year-End Offer for Your 2026 Budget

ToddySoft Connect 1.3.0 adds IEC 60870-5-104 and IEC 61850 modules for Ignition, triggered groups, device discovery on Ignition 8.3 and connectors for Apache Kafka Connect. New licenses ordered by December 18, 2026 include twelve months of maintenance and 10% off.

ToddySoft Connect 1.3.0 and ToddySoft Integrations 1.3.0 were released on November 13, 2026. This release brings two new modules for energy and substations on Ignition by Inductive Automation®, new ways to read and find devices, and connectors for Apache Kafka® Connect. The Adapters for Apache StreamPipes™ come with the 1.3.0 drivers too.

Ignition 8.1 and 8.3

Two new modules: IEC 60870-5-104 and IEC 61850. Both are for substations, telecontrol and distribution networks.

  • The IEC 60870-5-104 driver makes Ignition the controlling station for RTUs, substation gateways and telecontrol units. It delivers reports as the station sends them, with the station’s own timestamps and quality. The quality bits can also be tags of their own.
  • The IEC 61850 driver is an MMS client for IEDs: protection relays, bay controllers, meters and gateways. It reads and writes data attributes, operates control objects with their own control model and can take values from the IED’s report control blocks instead of polling them. The data model is browsed from the IED itself, so you need no SCD or ICD file.

Triggered groups. A group of tags is read together whenever a trigger tag changes, instead of at a fixed polling rate. This works in every module except the two IEC drivers, which already deliver reports as they arrive.

Device discovery on Ignition 8.3. Modbus/TCP and UMAS devices now show up in the Discover Devices dialog. If Ignition runs in Docker, the ToddySoft discovery agent finds devices on the plant network for it.

License diagnosis. The gateway shows why a device is or is not licensed and creates a report you can send to our support. On 8.1 you find it under Status > Other. On 8.3 it is a License card in the device form.

Requirement change. The Ignition 8.1 modules now need Ignition 8.1.33 or later (Java 17).

Beyond Ignition

Adapters for Apache StreamPipes. Every driver runs as its own extension service next to your StreamPipes installation: Beckhoff ADS, Siemens S7, Siemens S7 Web API, Bosch Rexroth ctrlX, EtherNet/IP, Schneider Electric UMAS, Modbus and OPC UA. See the StreamPipes page.

Connectors for Apache Kafka Connect. Seven drivers now come as source and sink connectors: Beckhoff ADS, Siemens S7, Siemens S7 Web API, Bosch Rexroth ctrlX, EtherNet/IP, Schneider Electric UMAS and Modbus. Each ships as a plugin archive and as a Confluent Hub component archive. They run on self-managed Kafka Connect workers (Apache Kafka 3.9 and 4.0 to 4.4) and on Confluent Platform 7.9 and 8.0 to 8.4. See the Kafka Connect page.

Manuals in English and German. Every module, adapter and connector ships with its manual, and every Ignition module also ships with release notes in both languages. They are linked from the product pages.
